Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners buy property in Mexico?
Yes! Foreign buyers can purchase property in Mexico through a fideicomiso (bank trust), which provides secure ownership. I'll guide you through this straightforward process.
Is financing available?
While Mexican bank financing for foreigners is limited, there are international financing options and developer financing programs available. I can connect you with trusted lenders.
What's included in the turnkey package?
The home comes fully furnished with all appliances, furniture, kitchenware, linens, and decor. Simply bring your suitcase and move in!
How long does the buying process take?
Typically 60-90 days from offer acceptance to closing. This includes due diligence, title work, and setting up the fideicomiso trust.
Are there rental restrictions?
FIDEPAZ allows short-term rentals, making this an excellent investment property. Many owners use their home part-time and generate income when away.
What about utilities and internet?
All utilities (water, electricity, gas) are established. High-speed fiber internet is available throughout FIDEPAZ, perfect for remote work.
